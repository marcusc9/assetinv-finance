Digital First, Human always means deliberately selective service.

Hybrid versus split coverage, minimum viable shelf versus partner-led adjacencies, and sector expansion must improve loyalty, productivity and risk-adjusted returns.

Customer Service Strategy

Serve smarter: route work by customer value, bank value, complexity and risk.

Recent UK evidence shows four service archetypes: digital-first banks lead ease, challengers lead visible ownership, incumbents lead breadth and specialists lead purpose. Group/CCB should combine the pieces it can credibly own: digital transparency, named human ownership for exceptions and ESG/sector insight in chosen segments.

Standout Service Fairness and Ethics Digital First Human always Sustainability
01 / Incumbents + global

Breadth, portals and product depth

NatWest, HSBC and Santander signal RMs, cash management, trade/FX, international tools and sustainability support.

Gap to fill: simpler ownership, onboarding status and fewer better journeys.
02 / Relationship challengers

Named banker, faster help

Allica markets a named relationship manager as standard; Handelsbanken leads the Ipsos/CMA RM/account management score at 85%.

Gap to fill: "known by your bank" service in selected Group-aligned segments.
03 / Neobanks + fintech

Digital ease and workflow

Ipsos/CMA leaders score 81-85% overall and 84-90% online/mobile; Mettle, Starling and Tide show accounting/invoicing workflow.

Gap to fill: digital onboarding, self-serve status and human escalation for risk.
04 / Purpose specialists

Values-led relevance

CAF Bank cites 14k+ charity/social-purpose customers and GBP1.45bn deposits; Triodos focuses on sustainable organisations.

Gap to fill: ESG insight and tools that help customers act, not generic ethics.
Match the market

Digital basics

Self-serve routine contacts, digital KYB/KYC, clear portal status and reusable documents.

Own the gap

Named case ownership

CSM/RS workflow for mandates, signatories, onboarding and urgent service failures.

Protect value

Human always, by exception

RM time focused on risk, retention, renewals, pricing, growth and sector advice.

Differentiate

ESG and sector insight

Sector playbooks, eligibility prompts and impact/data tags for chosen segments.

AI role in the model: classify intent flag repeat contacts detect risk and attrition signals summarise customer history suggest next best action draft customer updates

Customer Service Tactical Plan

Fix the four journeys that create effort, leakage and weak customer confidence.

The 2027 build should be informed by practical fixes now. Each journey needs one owner, a visible customer promise, a data baseline and a "do nothing" risk case.

01 Join

Onboarding

Owner Account Opening + CSM/RM

Current friction
Manual KYB/KYC, repeat asks, unclear status, weak ownership.
0-90 day fix
Stage map, document checklist, case owner, customer update cadence.
2027 acceleration
Digital KYB/KYC, document reuse, portal status, AI doc checks.
02 Protect

Risk / covenant monitoring

Owner Frontline + Credit/Risk

Current friction
Monitoring is more internal than customer-facing; weak early flags.
0-90 day fix
Covenant calendar, risk trigger list, RM/CSM review rhythm.
2027 acceleration
AI flags, CLMS/Salesforce data, proactive customer interventions.
03 Retain

Renewals

Owner Frontline + Portfolio/CSM

Current friction
Reactive activity, pricing/value not framed early enough.
0-90 day fix
120-day renewal queue, exception routing, pricing narrative.
2027 acceleration
Renewal cockpit with product, deposit, risk and service history.
04 Grow

Cross-sell + outreach

Owner Frontline + Marketing/Product

Current friction
No strong agenda that visibly helps customers or builds loyalty.
0-90 day fix
Sector campaign library, customer need prompts, marketing handoff.
2027 acceleration
Next-best-action journeys, ESG/product triggers, measured outreach.
If these journeys are not fixed: RM capacity remains trapped in admin service cost stays hidden deposits and fee income are missed competitors set the service bar

CCB / Group implication
UK incumbents

NatWest, Lloyds, Barclays, HSBC UK, Santander.

Breadth, product shelves, RMs, Bankline/HSBCnet-style portals, sector teams, cash management, sustainability and international tools.

Do not match breadth. Match clarity: fewer journeys, cleaner ownership, stronger digital basics and explicit service promises.

Challengers

Allica, OakNorth, Metro Bank.

Named bankers, faster onboarding, deposit rewards and practical workflow improvement; challenger/specialist banks accounted for 60% of gross SME bank lending in 2025, excluding overdrafts.

Commercial should naturally own "known by your bank" moments; remove admin drag before competitors make it the differentiator.

Neobanks / embedded

Monzo, Mettle, Starling, Tide, Zempler.

High digital advocacy, self-serve journeys, accounting integrations and transparent controls; UK open banking reached 16.5m user connections and 351m payments in 2025.

Digital First is now hygiene. Use Group scale and partners for routine servicing; save human time for risk and relationship value.

Global platforms

HSBC, Barclays Corporate, J.P. Morgan, Santander Navigator.

Treasury, payments, FX, trade, cross-border support and insight-led international growth propositions packaged as tools, not product lists.

Partner or route where needs exceed scale; avoid building a global bank for a small Commercial balance-sheet share.

Specialists

Handelsbanken, CAF Bank, Triodos, Charity Bank.

Relationship discretion, local or sector specialism, charity/social enterprise expertise and values-led/sustainable finance credibility.

Differentiate through chosen segments, ESG utility and human judgement; use Finalta/Savanta to evidence which niches merit 2027 funding.

Sources: Ipsos/CMA GB BCA Service Quality, February 2026; British Business Bank 2025/26; Open Banking Limited 2025; official provider pages.

2027 Focus Areas

Turn the strategy into four fundable 2027 decisions.

For CCO LT, Finance, Risk, Strategy, COO and Insight: the 2027 case should force a funding conversation on what Commercial must own, what Group can absorb, what needs evidence and where suppliers de-risk the build.

Own where differentiated

Relationship judgement, sector advice, risk exceptions and ESG-led customer insight.

Reuse where scale exists

Group contact centres, Business Banking patterns, enterprise platforms and shared data.

Fund where proved

Use Finalta, Savanta, Red Blue and controlled pilots to prove demand, value and delivery route.

2027 choice 0-90 day proof Funding ask + evidence Investment condition
01

Business operating model

Hybrid coverage or clearer origination, execution and portfolio ownership?

Prove

Contact taxonomy, cost-to-serve, RM/CSM/RS handoffs and customer pain by journey.

2027 ask

Commercial service pod, case ownership, AI triage and RM capacity release; Red Blue CX/service design input.

Decision gate

Approve the coverage split, journey owner and Group-versus-Commercial boundary.

02

Product set

Minimum viable shelf versus partner-led adjacencies?

Prove

Customer demand, revenue pool, risk fit and partner economics before build.

2027 ask

Protect core; test asset finance, invoice finance, cards/payments, cash management and FX; Savanta demand research.

Decision gate

Fund build, partner or stop choices only where demand, margin, conduct and risk fit are evidenced.

03

Sector focus

Which adjacencies beyond the current five have a right to win?

Prove

Score three hypotheses: purpose economy, professional services and retrofit/transition supply chain.

2027 ask

Two to three segment plays with risk playbooks and targeted campaigns; Finalta c.GBP70-150k benchmark plus Savanta sizing.

Decision gate

Fund only where stable liabilities, acceptable credit, service economics, product need and ESG/brand fit are quantified.

04

ESG proposition

How do we help clients turn ESG expectations into practical insight, funding and action?

Customer gap

Clients face ESG evidence requests, but lack simple data, sector benchmarks, eligibility guidance and funding pathways.

2027 ask

Sector playbooks, RM prompts, impact tags, eligibility tools and sustainable finance guidance; Savanta ESG client research.

Decision gate

Fund ESG only where customer utility, data availability, advice controls and product relevance are proved.

Funding gate

Only move to 2027 build funding where the choice has a named owner, validated customer need, supplier/research evidence, quantified cost-to-serve implication, conduct/risk controls and a practical delivery route.

Acceleration Plan

Make the 2027 case investable by proving the service model before funding the full build.

The near-term work is not a parallel strategy project; it is evidence generation for the Board case: customer friction, cost-to-serve, RM capacity, growth signals and data readiness.

Next 30 days Define the model

Agree service segmentation, contact taxonomy, target customers, journey owners, data gaps and supplier evidence brief.

60-90 days Stabilise journeys

Launch journey fixes; commission Finalta, Savanta and Red Blue inputs where they de-risk 2027 choices.

H2 2026 Run controlled pilots

Commercial service pod, AI triage, RM briefs, sector campaigns, CLMS/Salesforce journey data design.

2027 Fund the scalable build

Digital onboarding/service portal, RM cockpit, customer 360, ESG data/tools and product/partner propositions.

Decisions needed

Target customer ambition; service model; operating model; sector expansion; product build/partner choices; ESG data standard.

Evidence needed

Commercial NPS, contact reasons, journey times, activity logic, sector liability view, Finalta benchmark, Savanta customer research and Red Blue CX input.

Do not overbuild

Use Group contact centres, Business Banking participation and enterprise platforms where they fit; reserve bespoke Commercial investment for specialist risk.
