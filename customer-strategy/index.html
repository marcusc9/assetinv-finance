1
Current State

Commercial is strategically valuable, but the service model is under-defined.

Commercial is only c.1-2% of Group scale, so the answer needs to be selective: use Group capability where possible, add specialist Commercial capability only where customer value, risk or relationship value demands it.

What we currently know
Scale

Commercial is a small share of Group balance sheet and customer base, but has reputational and relationship value.

Service value set

No clearly documented Commercial service mindset, promise or ownership model.

Portfolios

RMs and CSMs watch their own books; cross-portfolio service learning is weak.

Growth

No strong cross-sell agenda that clearly helps customers and strengthens loyalty.

Digital

Weak Commercial digital capability; Salesforce is not yet a customer operating model.

Measurement

CB-specific NPS, activity logic, ESG tags and journey timing data are incomplete or unclear.

Executive implication

Reduce complexity without lowering the ambition.

  • Service cost: routine work must not consume RM capacity.
  • Trust: high-stress moments need named ownership, not channel handoff.
  • Investment: 2027 funding needs a customer case, not a technology shopping list.
  • Differentiation: Group values only matter if they shape service, advice and sector choices.
Working thesis

Commercial should be relationship-led by exception and digitally/operationally efficient by default.

2
Customer Service Strategy

Serve smarter: route work by customer value, bank value, complexity and risk.

CCB's customer strategy should be a focused growth agenda, not a broad rebuild: concentrate on the commercial segments where Group's trust, ethics and purpose-led heritage give it permission to win, then make the service model simple enough to scale. The 2027 investment case should fund the basics that unlock that ambition: digital onboarding, standout client servicing, clearer RM/CSM/RS ownership and ESG-led insight where the market is still under-served.

Standout Service Fairness and Ethics Digital First Human always Sustainability

Routine, low-complexity contacts

Statements, balance queries, simple updates, generic account help.

Route: digital, FAQs, Group contact centre, standard scripts.
02 / Commercial service pod

Business-specific servicing

Mandates, signatories, business documentation, onboarding status, operational fulfilment.

Route: CSM/RS workflow with clear case ownership.

High-friction or high-stress moments

Delayed onboarding, blocked transactions, complaints, vulnerability, urgent service failures.

Route: named owner, proactive updates, escalation standards.

High relationship value

Growth needs, retention risk, renewals, pricing, complex lending, sector/ESG advice.

Route: RM with AI brief, next best action and product/specialist support.

Fix the four journeys that create effort, leakage and weak customer confidence.

The 2027 build should be informed by practical fixes now. Each journey needs one owner, a visible customer promise, a data baseline and a "do nothing" risk case.

Enterprise Comparison

The competitor bar is no longer just lending appetite; it is service, workflow and insight.

Incumbents bring breadth and platforms; specialists bring focus and speed; fintech-led banks remove workflow friction. Group/CBS needs a selective counter-position anchored in trust and proportional delivery.

Peer signal
What they appear to be teaching the market
Implication for Group/CBS
NatWest / Lloyds / Barclays

Relationship teams, broad product shelves, digital portals, accounting/cashflow tools, sustainability hubs and sector expertise.

Do not match breadth; match clarity. Provide fewer, sharper customer journeys and a visible service promise.

Santander

Navigator Global positions tools, insights and expert support around international growth in one place.

Proposition packaging matters: customers respond to guided journeys, not product lists.

Allica

Focused on established SMEs with named relationship managers, rewards, assisted onboarding and integrated account features.

Challengers are selling the "known by your bank" experience that Commercial should own naturally.

OakNorth

Frames SME need around less fragmentation, cashflow visibility and finance workflows rather than banking alone.

Compete by reducing customer workflow friction, not only by improving internal banking processes.

Fintech / embedded finance

Set expectations for speed, transparency, pre-filled journeys, API connectivity and data-led prompts.

Partner or modular-build where needed; avoid heavy proprietary build for a 1-2% business line.

Where Group/CBS can win

Purpose-led customers, ethical trust, specialist relationship moments, Commercial service accountability.

Where Group/CBS should not overcompete

Full universal-bank product breadth, deep proprietary workflow platforms, high-cost bespoke servicing for routine contacts.

5
2027 Focus Areas

Four choices need resolution before the 2027 investment case can be credible.

This is where the pack should generate executive debate: what should Commercial Bank be excellent at, what should it partner for, and what should it stop trying to carry internally?

01

Business operating model

Decide between a hybrid RM/CSM model and a clearer origination + execution + portfolio management split.

  • RMs: origination, advice, retention, complex judgement.
  • CSMs: service ownership, onboarding orchestration, complex cases.
  • RS: execution factory for mandates, signatures and fulfilment.
02

Product set

Clarify the minimum viable product shelf before adding propositions.

  • Core: BCA, savings, overdraft, loans, commercial mortgages.
  • Adjacency: asset finance, invoice finance, cards/payments, cash management, FX.
  • Route: build only if scale supports it; partner where capability burden is high.
03

Sector focus beyond the current five

Current focus: Social Housing, Trading, Healthcare, Renewables, Commercial Real Estate.

  • Adjacency candidates: charities/NFP, education, professional services, community infrastructure.
  • Selective tests: clean transport/logistics, food/agri, tech-enabled services.
  • Decision lens: values fit, deposit stability, credit insight, product relevance.
04

ESG proposition, tools, insight, data

Move ESG from policy language to customer help and portfolio insight.

  • Sector playbooks and RM prompts.
  • Impact/ESG tags for new business pipeline.
  • Tools for customer transition needs and sustainable finance eligibility.
6
Acceleration Plan

Make the 2027 case investable by proving the service model before funding the full build.

The near-term work is not a parallel strategy project; it is evidence generation for the Board case: customer friction, cost-to-serve, RM capacity, growth signals and data readiness.

Next 30 days Define the model

Agree service segmentation, contact taxonomy, target customers, journey owners and current data gaps.

60-90 days Stabilise journeys

Launch onboarding, renewal and covenant fixes; codify CSM/RS/RM handoffs; create outreach logic.

H2 2026 Run controlled pilots

Commercial service pod, AI triage, RM briefs, sector campaigns, CLMS/Salesforce journey data design.

2027 Fund the scalable build

Digital onboarding/service portal, RM cockpit, customer 360, ESG data/tools and product/partner propositions.

Decisions needed

Target customer ambition; service model; operating model; sector expansion; product build/partner choices; ESG data standard.

Evidence needed

Commercial NPS baseline, contact reasons, journey times, activity logic, sector liability view, ESG/impact tagging, fee-income clarity.

Do not overbuild

Use Group contact centres and enterprise platforms where they fit; reserve bespoke Commercial investment for high-value moments and specialist risk.

7
Investment Shape

Put numbers around the ask before requesting the full 2027 build.

Indicative planning ranges for Finance validation only. The immediate case should fund evidence and supplier discovery, then gate any larger 2027 build through proven customer need, cost-to-serve and risk control.

2026 evidence budget c. GBP 0.5m-1.2m

Research, service design, data baselining, vendor discovery and controlled pilots.

2027 build options Gate before committing

Light orchestration of Group platforms first; larger digital builds only if evidence supports scale.

Supplier principle Buy insight, not dependency

Use suppliers to accelerate specialist work, with Group retaining customer ownership and standards.
